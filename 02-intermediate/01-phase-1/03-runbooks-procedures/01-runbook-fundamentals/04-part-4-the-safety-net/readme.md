## 🚀 Interview Preparation

Be prepared to answer these "Staff-Level" questions:
1.  **"How do you handle secrets during a runbook execution?"** (Ans: Never put them in the doc; link to a secret manager).
2.  **"How do you verify a runbook is still valid?"** (Ans: Periodic Gamedays and 'Last Used' timestamps).
3.  **"How do you reduce toil through documentation?"** (Ans: By creating 'Ready-to-Automate' steps).
