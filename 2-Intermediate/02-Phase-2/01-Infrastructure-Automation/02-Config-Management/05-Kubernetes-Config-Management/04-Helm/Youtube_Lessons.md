# 📺 YouTube Lessons: Helm Package Management

Learn to package, version, and share your Kubernetes applications using Helm.

---

## 📦 Helm Fundamentals

*   **[Introduction to Helm Charts for Beginners](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hiKPPy582vg)** by *Tech Tutorials with Piyush*
    *   *Focus*: Package management, components, and installing your first app.
*   **[You will never forget HELM after watching this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tK9Oc6AEnR4)** by *Abhishek.Veeramalla*
    *   *Focus*: Fundamentals, packaging, versioning, and upgrading apps.

---

## 🛠️ Chart Development

*   **[Introduction to Helm | Beginners Guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RR25S8UvPik)** by *Marcel Dempers*
    *   *Focus*: Templating, values, parameters, and chart lifecycle.
*   **[Helm and Helm Charts Explained](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gS8H0VvVfIs)**
    *   *Focus*: Complete theory of how Helm interacts with the K8s API.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip**: Always use `helm lint` and `helm install --dry-run --debug` before deploying a new chart to catch syntax errors early.
