# 06: Interview Questions and Quizzes

Mastering FinOps requires a balance of technical knowledge, financial literacy, and cultural change management.

## 🎤 Top 20 Interview Questions

1.  **What is FinOps, and why is it necessary for modern cloud environments?**
2.  **Explain the three phases of the FinOps lifecycle (Inform, Optimize, Operate).**
3.  **What is the difference between Showback and Chargeback?**
4.  **How do you handle 'Unallocated Costs' in a multi-tenant cloud environment?**
5.  **What are 'Unit Metrics' (e.g., Cost per Transaction), and why are they better than 'Total Spend'?**
6.  **Explain the 'Iron Triangle' of Cloud (Cost, Quality, Speed).**
7.  **What is a 'Tagging Policy', and how does it drive cost accountability?**
8.  **Compare Reserved Instances (RIs) with Savings Plans. When would you choose one over the other?**
9.  **What is 'Right-sizing', and what metrics do you look at to determine it?**
10. **How do you manage 'Idle Resources', and what automation can you implement?**
11. **What is the FinOps Foundation's definition of 'Cloud FinOps'?**
12. **Explain the 'Crawl, Walk, Run' maturity model.**
13. **What is 'Cost Avoidance' vs. 'Cost Savings'?**
14. **How do you build a business case for a FinOps team in an organization?**
15. **What is the primary role of a 'FinOps Practitioner'?**
16. **How do you handle 'Variable Spend' in a way that finance teams can understand?**
17. **What is a 'Cost Anomaly', and how do you detect and respond to it?**
18. **Explain the importance of 'Forecasting' in FinOps.**
19. **What is 'Spot Instance' usage, and what are the risks involved?**
20. **How do you foster a 'Culture of Accountability' among developers?**

## 📝 20-Question Knowledge Quiz

1.  **Which phase of the FinOps lifecycle focuses on visibility and allocation?**
    - A) Inform
    - B) Optimize
    - C) Operate
    - D) Implement

2.  **Chargeback is defined as:**
    - A) Showing a department their costs without billing them.
    - B) Actually billing a department's budget for their cloud usage.
    - C) Getting a refund from the cloud provider.
    - D) Reversing a transaction.

3.  **What is the primary goal of the 'Optimize' phase?**
    - A) Increasing spend
    - B) Finding opportunities to reduce waste and improve efficiency
    - C) Setting up dashboards
    - D) Hiring more engineers

4.  **A 'Shared Service' cost (like support fees) should ideally be allocated via:**
    - A) Giving it all to the IT department
    - B) Proportional allocation based on usage
    - C) Leaving it unallocated
    - D) Random distribution

5.  **Which commitment model is usually the most flexible for fluctuating compute needs?**
    - A) Standard RI
    - B) Convertible RI
    - C) Compute Savings Plan
    - D) Spot Instances

6.  **'Unit Economics' in FinOps refers to:**
    - A) The cost per individual server
    - B) The cost of cloud services per unit of business value (e.g., per order)
    - C) The price of a single GPU
    - D) The annual budget of the FinOps team

7.  **Right-sizing involves:**
    - A) Buying more servers
    - B) Matching instance types/sizes to actual workload requirements
    - C) Increasing the disk size of every database
    - D) Moving everything to serverless

8.  **What is a 'Cloud Bill' complexity factor?**
    - A) Millions of line items
    - B) Variable pricing models
    - C) Lack of metadata/tags
    - D) All of the above

9.  **The FinOps Foundation is part of the:**
    - A) Apache Foundation
    - B) Linux Foundation
    - C) CNCF
    - D) IEEE

10. **A 'Cost Anomaly' is most likely caused by:**
    - A) A successful marketing campaign
    - B) A misconfigured script or a security breach
    - C) Regular seasonal traffic
    - D) Both A and B

11. **Which persona is NOT typically part of a FinOps team?**
    - A) Engineering
    - B) Finance/Procurement
    - C) Graphic Design
    - D) Business/Product Owners

12. **What is 'Reserved Instance Coverage'?**
    - A) The percentage of your instances that are physical
    - B) The percentage of your total running hours covered by commitments
    - C) The amount of insurance you have for your data center
    - D) The number of regions you operate in

13. **Reducing 'Waste' in the cloud primarily targets:**
    - A) Unattached EBS volumes and Idle EC2 instances
    - B) The number of developers in the company
    - C) The length of Slack meetings
    - D) The use of open-source software

14. **'Forecasting' in FinOps is difficult because of:**
    - A) Cloud's on-demand nature
    - B) Rapid architectural changes
    - C) Lack of historical data
    - D) Both A and B

15. **What does the 'Crawl' stage of maturity imply?**
    - A) Basic reporting and small wins
    - B) Fully automated real-time optimization
    - C) No visibility into spend
    - D) Decommissioning the cloud

16. **A 'Tagging Strategy' is most effective when it is:**
    - A) Optional for developers
    - B) Consistent and enforced via policy/automation
    - C) Decided by the CEO only
    - D) Written in a PDF no one reads

17. **Spot Instances can be interrupted with a notice of \_\_\_\_\_\_ on AWS.**
    - A) 10 seconds
    - B) 2 minutes
    - C) 1 hour
    - D) 24 hours

18. **The primary benefit of 'Showback' is:**
    - A) Moving money between departments
    - B) Creating awareness and accountability without financial friction
    - C) Lowering the tax rate
    - D) Speeding up the network

19. **What is the 'Iron Triangle' constraint?**
    - A) You can't have speed and low cost without sacrificing quality
    - B) You can't have more than 3 developers per project
    - C) You can only use 3 cloud providers
    - D) None of the above

20. **FinOps is about making money, not just saving it.**
    - A) True
    - B) False
