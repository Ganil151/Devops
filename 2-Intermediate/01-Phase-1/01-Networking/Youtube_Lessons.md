# 📺 YouTube Lessons: Networking & VPC

This guide provides a curated list of high-quality YouTube tutorials to supplement your learning of AWS Networking and VPC concepts.

---

## 🏗️ VPC Basics & Subnetting

Modern infrastructure starts with a solid VPC design. These videos cover CIDR blocks, Internet Gateways, NAT Gateways, and Route Tables.

*   **[AWS VPC Deep Dive (Part 2) - Subnets, Routing, and Security](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hiKPPy582vg)** by *egain*
    *   *Focus*: In-depth look at public/private subnets and security.
*   **[AWS Networking Basics for Cloud Engineers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g2JOHLHh_J0)** by *Josh Geissler*
    *   *Focus*: Hands-on guide to configuring a VPC from scratch.
*   **[AWS VPC & Subnets for Beginners](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZNVfv0z6tg)** by *KodeKloud*
    *   *Focus*: Foundation of VPC, Subnets, and Connectivity.
*   **[AWS VPC, Subnets, Security Groups, NAT Gateway & IP Addresses](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RR25S8UvPik)**
    *   *Focus*: Comprehensive foundational concepts including AZs and Regions.

## 🔗 VPC Peering

Learn how to connect disparate VPCs and manage routing across them.

*   **[AWS VPC & VPC Peering Project for DevOps](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gS8H0VvVfIs)** by *TrainWithShubham*
    *   *Focus*: Practical project demonstrating cross-VPC communication.
*   **[VPC Peering in AWS - Hands-On Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H7-YyvL2z8k)**
    *   *Focus*: Setting up peering between two VPCs step-by-step.
*   **[AWS VPC Peering - Step By Step Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8-e-f2uLwA)** by *Rahul Wagh*
    *   *Focus*: Detailed configuration of peering connections.

## ⚖️ Load Balancing (ALB, NLB, GLB)

Distribute traffic efficiently across your instances and ensure high availability.

*   **[AWS Elastic Load Balancer (ELB) Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J8lS3A9O8O4)**
    *   *Focus*: Deep dive into different types of ELBs and hands-on setup.
*   **[Master All 3 AWS Load Balancers - Full Course](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_MUhD1p_pQ)** by *Rahul Wagh*
    *   *Focus*: Comprehensive comparison and setup of ALB, NLB, and GLB.
*   **[AWS Elastic Load Balancing Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7uV8-OQ9Dpw)** by *Stephane Maarek*
    *   *Focus*: Focuses on Application Load Balancers, target groups, and health checks.
