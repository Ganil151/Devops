# 📜 Module 01: Introduction to Policy

> **"If it isn't written down, it's just an opinion. If it's written in code, it's a guarantee."**

## 📚 Overview

Compliance often feels like a burden because it is traditionally manual. "Policy as Code" flips the script. We write rules (policies) that automated systems can read and enforce.

## 🎓 Learning Objectives

- ✅ **Governance**: Examples like "All data must be encrypted at rest."
- ✅ **Frameworks**: SOC2 (Trust), HIPAA (Health), PCI-DSS (Payments).
- ✅ **Shift Left**: Catching compliance violations in the Pull Request, not the audit.

---

## 🏗️ The Evolution of Policy

### 1. The Spreadsheet Era
- Audit every year.
- Manually check 500 servers.
- Result: "Compliance Drift" (Servers become non-compliant 5 minutes after the audit).

### 2. The Automation Era (Policy as Code)
- Audit every commit.
- Automatically check every Terraform plan.
- Result: **Continuous Compliance**.
