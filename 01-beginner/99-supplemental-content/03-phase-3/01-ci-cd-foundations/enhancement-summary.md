## 🏗️ Technical Standards (The "Engineer Way")

Every module in this phase must adhere to the following:

1.  **Junior vs Engineer Comparison**: Explicitly contrast manual "Junior" habits with automated "Engineer" patterns.
2.  **Infrastructure Analogies**: Use the "Software Factory" or "Automated Kitchen" mental models.
3.  **Visual Architecture**: Use Mermaid diagrams for workflow lifecycles, DAG execution, and Master/Agent communication.
4.  **Security-First**: Emphasize credential segregation, immutable artifacts, and "no-root" runner policies.
5.  **Fail-Fast Logic**: Explain why stopping a pipeline early is a victory, not a defeat.
