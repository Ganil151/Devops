# Module 12: Digital Assets & Passive Income

Build once, sell forever. This module covers creating digital products and leveraging affiliate networks to generate passive revenue streams.

---

## 📦 Digital Products (Templates & Kits)

DevOps engineers love not starting from zero. Sell the "starter kit" you wish you had.

### Ideas
1.  **Terraform Module Packs**: A production-ready VPC + EKS + RDS setup for AWS.
2.  **Notion Dashboards**: "The Ultimate DevOps Project Tracker" or "SRE Incident Response Template".
3.  **Obsidian Vaults**: A curated knowledge base for certification study (CKA, AWS SA).
4.  **Scripts**: Advanced bash/python automation scripts (e.g., "Automated Server Hardening Kit").

### Where to Sell
- **Gumroad**: The easiest place to start. Low fees, handles global tax.
- **Lemon Squeezy**: Merchant of Record (handles tax compliance). Great checkout UI.
- **Etsy**: Surprisingly good for digital planners and notion templates.

## 🤝 Affiliate Marketing

Earn a commission by recommending tools you use and trust.

### High-Value Programs
- **Hosting**: DigitalOcean, Linode, Vultr (often huge credits or cash payouts).
- **SaaS Tools**: Datadog, New Relic, PagerDuty often have partner programs.
- **Courses**: Coursera, Udemy, Pluralsight affiliates.

### Strategy (The "How-To" Method)
Don't just paste links. Write a high-quality tutorial (e.g., "How to set up K8s on DigitalOcean") and include your affiliate link naturally as the setup step.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Always disclose your affiliate links. It's a legal requirement (FTC) and builds trust with your audience.

## 💖 Sponsorships

If you create open-source software (OSS) or content, people may support you directly.

- **GitHub Sponsors**: The best option for code creators. 100% of the money often goes to you (GitHub covers fees).
- **Patreon**: Good for content creators (YouTubers, Podcasters).
- **Buy Me a Coffee**: Simple, low-friction tipping jar.

### How to get Sponsored
1.  **Solve a real problem**: Your tool must save companies time.
2.  **Documentation**: Excellent docs make people love your tool.
3.  **Consistency**: Show that the project is alive and maintained.
