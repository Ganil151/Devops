# 📝 Content Creation & Education
Scaling your knowledge by teaching others through various media.

1.  **Paid Technical Writing**: Create high-quality tutorials for vendor blogs (DigitalOcean, HashiCorp, CircleCI). These often pay between $300 and $800 per deep-dive article.
2.  **Comprehensive Online Courses**: Develop niche-focused courses on platforms like Udemy, Pluralsight, or your own site. Focus on emerging tech like "Cilium for Kubernetes Networking."
3.  **Niche Technical Blogging**: Build a personal brand through a blog. Monetize via high-quality display ads (Mediavine/AdThrive) or direct sponsorships from infrastructure tool vendors.
4.  **Educational YouTube Channel**: Share visual tutorials and "Day in the Life" content. Revenue comes from AdSense, affiliate links for cloud tools, and sponsored segments.
5.  **Technical E-books & Guides**: Self-publish deep-dive PDF guides or cookbooks (e.g., "The Terraform Production Checklist") on Gumroad or Leanpub.
6.  **Premium Newsletters**: Launch a Substack focusing on "DevOps Weekly Trends" or "Cloud Security Deep Dives" with a paid tier for exclusive architecture breakdowns.
