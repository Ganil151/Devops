# 💡 Knowledge Sharing & App Creation
Combining the power of community building with technical product launches.

1.  **Corporate Training Workshops**: Deliver 2-3 day intensive training sessions for engineering teams transitioning to Kubernetes, Serverless, or GitOps.
2.  **1-on-1 Paid Mentorship**: Offer career coaching and technical guidance for junior engineers on platforms like MentorCruise or ADPList (premium tier).
3.  **Paid Professional Communities**: Create a gated Discord or Slack community where senior engineers share production "war stories" and architectural advice.
4.  **Specialized Micro-SaaS Apps**: Build small, focused web apps that solve DevOps tasks, like a "Cron Job Monitor" or a "K8s Cost Estimator" with a subscription model.
5.  **DevOps for Web3/Blockchain**: Provide specialized "Node-Management-as-a-Service" or validator infrastructure for blockchain networks, charging a fee or percentage of staking rewards.
6.  **Technical Advisory for VCs**: Help Venture Capital firms perform technical due diligence on infrastructure and cloud-based startups they are considering for investment.
