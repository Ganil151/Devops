# 🎯 Niche & Strategic Roles
Specialized high-value activities that leverage your strategic understanding of the ecosystem.

1.  **Fractional CTO / DevOps Advisor**: Act as a part-time technical leader for startups that need senior guidance on infrastructure strategy but aren't ready for a full-time hire.
2.  **Technical Recruiting & Vetting**: Partner with HR firms to conduct deep technical interviews for DevOps/SRE roles, ensuring they hire high-quality talent.
3.  **Developer Relations (DevRel) Consulting**: Help DevOps tool startups improve their documentation, community engagement, and product feedback loops.
4.  **DevSecOps Strategy**: Focus exclusively on the "security" in DevOps. Implement SAST/DAST, secret management, and compliance-as-code (SOC2/HIPAA) for fintech/healthtech.
5.  **Platform Engineering Consulting**: Help mid-to-large enterprises build Internal Developer Platforms (IDP) using Backstage or custom portals to reduce developer friction.
6.  **Vulnerability Research**: Participate in private bug bounty programs specifically targeting infrastructure tools and cloud providers.
