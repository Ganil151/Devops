# 30-Day Action Plan: Launch Knowledge Sharing & Apps

Launch your training, mentorship, or micro-SaaS business and acquire your first paying customers in 30 days.

---

## 📊 30-Day Overview

| Week | Focus | Goal |
|------|-------|------|
| **Week 1** | Choose Path & Create Offering | Service/product defined, materials ready |
| **Week 2** | Platform Setup & Marketing | Profiles live, landing page created |
| **Week 3** | Free Sessions & Validation | 3-5 free sessions, testimonials collected |
| **Week 4** | Launch & First Sales | First 5-10 paying customers |

---

## Week 1: Define Your Offering (Days 1-7)

### Day 1-2: Choose Your Path
- [ ] Decide: Training, Mentorship, Community, or Micro-SaaS
- [ ] Review [Getting Started Guide](../02-Consulting-Services/Getting-Started.md)
- [ ] Choose based on:
  - Your strengths (teaching vs. building)
  - Time available
  - Revenue goals

---

### Day 3-5: Create Your Offering

**If Training**:
- [ ] Choose workshop topic (e.g., "Kubernetes for Production")
- [ ] Create outline (6-8 modules)
- [ ] Develop 2-3 hands-on labs
- [ ] Price: $8k-15k for 2-day workshop

**If Mentorship**:
- [ ] Define packages:
  - Career coaching: $1,200 for 3 months
  - Technical mentorship: $800/month
- [ ] Create intake questionnaire
- [ ] Set up [Calendly](https://calendly.com)

**If Community**:
- [ ] Define niche: "Senior DevOps Engineers in Fintech"
- [ ] Choose platform: Slack or Discord
- [ ] Plan content calendar (weekly office hours, AMAs)
- [ ] Price: $50-100/month

**If Micro-SaaS**:
- [ ] Follow [Tools 30-Day Plan](./03-Tools-30-Day-Plan.md) for validation and building

---

### Day 6-7: Create Marketing Materials
- [ ] Write service description
- [ ] Create simple landing page ([Carrd](https://carrd.co))
- [ ] Design pricing tiers
- [ ] Prepare testimonial requests

---

## Week 2: Platform Setup (Days 8-14)

### Day 8-10: Set Up Platforms

**For Mentorship**:
- [ ] Create profile on [MentorCruise](https://mentorcruise.com)
- [ ] Set up on [ADPList](https://adplist.org)
- [ ] Add to LinkedIn Services

**For Training**:
- [ ] Create [Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com) page
- [ ] Set up [Teachable](https://teachable.com) or [Thinkific](https://www.thinkific.com)

**For Community**:
- [ ] Set up Discord/Slack workspace
- [ ] Create [Circle](https://circle.so) or [Mighty Networks](https://www.mightynetworks.com) community
- [ ] Configure payment via [Stripe](https://stripe.com)

**Tools**: [MentorCruise](https://mentorcruise.com), [Teachable](https://teachable.com), [Circle](https://circle.so)

---

### Day 11-12: Create Content
- [ ] Write 3 LinkedIn posts about your topic
- [ ] Create lead magnet (cheat sheet, checklist)
- [ ] Record intro video using [Loom](https://www.loom.com)

---

### Day 13-14: Build Email List
- [ ] Set up [ConvertKit](https://convertkit.com)
- [ ] Create signup form with lead magnet
- [ ] Add to landing page and LinkedIn
- [ ] Goal: 50 signups

---

## Week 3: Free Sessions & Validation (Days 15-21)

### Day 15-17: Offer 3 Free Sessions
- [ ] Post on LinkedIn: "Offering 3 free [mentorship/training] sessions"
- [ ] Conduct sessions and over-deliver
- [ ] Take detailed notes on what resonates

---

### Day 18-19: Collect Testimonials
- [ ] Email participants asking for testimonials
- [ ] Request LinkedIn recommendations
- [ ] Use template:

```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for the [session/workshop]! I'd love to hear your feedback.

Would you mind writing a brief testimonial about:
- What problem we solved
- What you learned
- What results you've seen

I'll feature it on my website and LinkedIn.

Thanks!
[Your Name]
```

---

### Day 20-21: Refine Offering
- [ ] Incorporate feedback
- [ ] Adjust pricing if needed
- [ ] Finalize service packages

---

## Week 4: Launch & First Sales (Days 22-30)

### Day 22-23: Launch Announcement
- [ ] Write launch post for LinkedIn
- [ ] Email your list
- [ ] Share in DevOps communities
- [ ] Offer early-bird discount (20-30% off)

---

### Day 24-26: Conduct Discovery Calls
- [ ] Schedule calls with interested leads
- [ ] Present your offering
- [ ] Handle objections
- [ ] Close first 3-5 customers

---

### Day 27-28: Onboard First Customers
- [ ] Send welcome email
- [ ] Schedule first session/grant access
- [ ] Set expectations
- [ ] Over-deliver on value

---

### Day 29-30: Reflect & Scale
- [ ] Review metrics:
  - Customers: 5-10
  - Revenue: $500-5,000
  - Testimonials: 3-5
- [ ] Plan next 30 days:
  - Scale to 20 customers
  - Automate onboarding
  - Create more content

---

## 📊 Success Metrics

By Day 30:
- ✅ Service/product launched
- ✅ 3-5 free sessions delivered
- ✅ 3-5 testimonials collected
- ✅ 5-10 paying customers
- ✅ $500-5,000 revenue
