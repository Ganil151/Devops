# 30-Day Action Plan: Position Yourself for Strategic Roles

Land your first fractional CTO, technical advisory, or strategic consulting engagement in 30 days.

---

## 📊 30-Day Overview

| Week | Focus | Goal |
|------|-------|------|
| **Week 1** | Authority Building | 5 thought leadership posts, portfolio complete |
| **Week 2** | Strategic Networking | 20 connections with VCs/CTOs |
| **Week 3** | Service Packaging & Outreach | Packages defined, 10 discovery calls |
| **Week 4** | Close First Engagement | 1-2 strategic clients signed |

---

## Week 1: Build Authority (Days 1-7)

### Day 1-2: Create Strategic Case Studies
- [ ] Document 3 strategic projects (not just technical):
  - Business impact (revenue, cost savings, risk reduction)
  - Decision-making process
  - Stakeholder management
- [ ] Quantify outcomes in business terms

---

### Day 3-5: Write 5 Thought Leadership Posts
- [ ] Post topics:
  1. "5 Infrastructure Decisions That Cost Startups Millions"
  2. "How to Evaluate DevOps Tools: A Framework for CTOs"
  3. "The Hidden Costs of DIY Infrastructure"
  4. "When NOT to Use Kubernetes"
  5. "Building vs. Buying: A Decision Matrix"
- [ ] Publish on LinkedIn (one per day for Days 3-7)

**Tools**: LinkedIn, [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com)

---

### Day 6-7: Optimize for Strategic Positioning
- [ ] Update LinkedIn headline: "Fractional CTO | DevOps Strategy | Helping Startups Scale Infrastructure"
- [ ] Rewrite About section with business outcomes
- [ ] Add "Open to Fractional CTO Opportunities" to profile

---

## Week 2: Strategic Networking (Days 8-14)

### Day 8-10: Build Target List
- [ ] Use [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com) to find:
  - Recently funded startups (Series A-B)
  - 20-100 employees
  - Your target industry
- [ ] Create list of 30 companies
- [ ] Find CTOs/VPs on LinkedIn

**Tools**: [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com), [LinkedIn Sales Navigator](https://business.linkedin.com/sales-solutions/sales-navigator) (free trial)

---

### Day 11-12: Connect with 20 Decision Makers
- [ ] Send personalized connection requests:

```
Hi [Name],

Congrats on [Company]'s recent [funding/launch]!

I help companies at your stage [specific outcome]. Would love to connect and share insights from working with [similar companies].

Best,
[Your Name]
```

- [ ] Don't pitch yet - just connect

---

### Day 13-14: Engage with VC Networks
- [ ] Join [CTO Craft](https://ctocraft.com) or similar communities
- [ ] Connect with 5 VCs on LinkedIn
- [ ] Offer value: "Happy to provide technical due diligence for your portfolio companies"

**Tools**: [CTO Craft](https://ctocraft.com), LinkedIn

---

## Week 3: Package & Outreach (Days 15-21)

### Day 15-16: Define Service Packages
- [ ] Create 3 service offerings:

**Fractional CTO**:
- 15 hours/week, $12k-20k/month
- Infrastructure strategy, hiring, board updates

**Technical Advisory**:
- 5 hours/week, $5k-8k/month
- Architecture reviews, tool selection, vendor negotiations

**Strategic Audit**:
- One-time, $5k-10k
- Infrastructure assessment, roadmap, recommendations

---

### Day 17-18: Create Proposal Templates
- [ ] Write templates for each service
- [ ] Include: Scope, deliverables, timeline, pricing
- [ ] Add case studies and testimonials

**Tools**: [Bonsai](https://www.hellobonsai.com), Google Docs

---

### Day 19-20: Direct Outreach (10 Companies)
- [ ] Email template:

```
Subject: Quick question about [Company]'s infrastructure strategy

Hi [CTO Name],

Congrats on the recent Series A!

I specialize in helping companies at your stage build scalable infrastructure without over-engineering. Recently helped [Similar Company] reduce cloud costs by 40% while improving deployment speed.

Would you be open to a 20-minute call to discuss your infrastructure roadmap?

Best,
[Your Name]
[LinkedIn] | [Case Studies]
```

- [ ] Send to 10 companies
- [ ] Follow up after 3 days

---

### Day 21: Conduct 3-5 Discovery Calls
- [ ] Use discovery call script from [Getting Started Guide](../04-Niche-Roles/Getting-Started.md)
- [ ] Focus on business outcomes, not just technical details
- [ ] Take detailed notes

---

## Week 4: Close Deals (Days 22-30)

### Day 22-24: Send Proposals
- [ ] Create detailed proposals for 2-3 best discovery calls
- [ ] Include:
  - Executive summary
  - Scope of work
  - Timeline and milestones
  - Pricing options
- [ ] Send within 24 hours of call

---

### Day 25-27: Follow Up & Negotiate
- [ ] Follow up on proposals after 2-3 days
- [ ] Be prepared to:
  - Adjust scope
  - Offer payment plans
  - Start with smaller engagement

---

### Day 28: Close First Engagement
- [ ] Finalize contract
- [ ] Set up payment (50% upfront for projects, monthly for retainers)
- [ ] Schedule kickoff call

**Tools**: [DocuSign](https://www.docusign.com), [Stripe](https://stripe.com)

---

### Day 29-30: Deliver Initial Value
- [ ] Conduct kickoff and initial assessment
- [ ] Provide quick wins in first week
- [ ] Set up regular communication cadence

---

## 📊 Success Metrics

By Day 30:
- ✅ 5 thought leadership posts published
- ✅ 20+ strategic connections made
- ✅ 10 discovery calls conducted
- ✅ 2-3 proposals sent
- ✅ 1-2 strategic engagements signed
- ✅ $10k-30k in contracts
