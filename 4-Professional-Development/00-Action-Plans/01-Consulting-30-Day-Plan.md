# 30-Day Action Plan: Launch Your DevOps Consulting Practice

This is your day-by-day roadmap to landing your first consulting clients and generating $2k-5k in revenue within 30 days.

---

## 📊 30-Day Overview

| Week | Focus | Goal |
|------|-------|------|
| **Week 1** | Foundation & Portfolio | 3 case studies, LinkedIn optimized |
| **Week 2** | Platform Setup & Outreach | Profiles live, 10 proposals sent |
| **Week 3** | Proposals & Discovery Calls | 5 discovery calls, 2 proposals |
| **Week 4** | Close Deals & Deliver | 2-3 clients signed, first project started |

---

## Week 1: Foundation & Portfolio (Days 1-7)

### Day 1: Self-Assessment & Niche Selection
**Time**: 2-3 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Complete the self-assessment in the [Getting Started Guide](../02-Consulting-Services/Getting-Started.md)
- [ ] Choose your consulting niche (Kubernetes, FinOps, DevSecOps, etc.)
- [ ] List 5 past projects that demonstrate your expertise
- [ ] Identify your ideal client profile (startup size, industry, tech stack)

**Tools**:
- Notion or Google Docs for documentation

**Success Criteria**: Clear niche defined, 5 projects identified

---

### Day 2: Create Case Study #1
**Time**: 3-4 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Choose your strongest project
- [ ] Write case study using this template:

```markdown
## Project: [Industry] - [Problem Solved]

**Client**: [Company type, size] (anonymize if needed)
**Challenge**: [What was broken/needed]
**My Role**: [What you did specifically]
**Solution**: [Technologies and approach used]
**Results**: 
- [Quantifiable metric 1: e.g., "Reduced deployment time by 75%"]
- [Quantifiable metric 2: e.g., "Cut cloud costs by $40k/year"]
- [Quantifiable metric 3: e.g., "Achieved 99.9% uptime"]

**Technologies**: Kubernetes, Terraform, AWS, Prometheus, etc.
```

**Tools**:
- Google Docs or Notion
- [Hemingway Editor](http://hemingwayapp.com/) for clarity

**Success Criteria**: One complete case study with metrics

---

### Day 3: Create Case Studies #2 & #3
**Time**: 4-5 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Write second case study (different problem/technology)
- [ ] Write third case study (showcases breadth)
- [ ] Ensure each highlights different skills:
  - Case Study 1: Technical depth (e.g., Kubernetes migration)
  - Case Study 2: Business impact (e.g., cost optimization)
  - Case Study 3: Problem-solving (e.g., incident resolution)

**Success Criteria**: Three diverse case studies complete

---

### Day 4: Optimize LinkedIn Profile
**Time**: 2-3 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Update headline: `"DevOps Consultant | [Your Niche] Specialist | Helping Companies [Specific Outcome]"`
  - Example: `"DevOps Consultant | Kubernetes & AWS Specialist | Helping Startups Cut Cloud Costs by 40%"`
- [ ] Rewrite About section:
  - Lead with results: "I've helped 10+ companies..."
  - Showcase expertise: "Specializing in..."
  - Call to action: "Looking to optimize your infrastructure? Let's talk."
- [ ] Add Featured section with case studies
- [ ] Update Experience with quantifiable achievements
- [ ] Add Skills: Kubernetes, Terraform, AWS, CI/CD, etc.

**Tools**:
- [LinkedIn Profile Checklist](https://www.linkedin.com/business/sales/blog/profile-best-practices/17-steps-to-a-better-linkedin-profile-in-2017)

**Success Criteria**: Professional LinkedIn profile optimized for consulting

---

### Day 5: Create GitHub Portfolio
**Time**: 2-3 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Create a portfolio repository: `devops-portfolio`
- [ ] Add README with:
  - Brief bio
  - Areas of expertise
  - Links to case studies
  - Sample code/configurations
- [ ] Upload 3-5 sample projects:
  - Terraform modules
  - CI/CD pipeline examples
  - Monitoring configurations
  - Scripts or automation tools
- [ ] Ensure all code is well-documented

**Tools**:
- [GitHub](https://github.com)
- [GitHub Profile README Generator](https://rahuldkjain.github.io/gh-profile-readme-generator/)

**Success Criteria**: Professional GitHub portfolio live

---

### Day 6: Build Simple Website (Optional but Recommended)
**Time**: 3-4 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Choose platform:
  - [Carrd](https://carrd.co) (easiest, $19/year)
  - [Webflow](https://webflow.com) (more features, free tier)
  - [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/) + [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) (free, technical)
- [ ] Create 3 pages:
  - **Home**: Value proposition, brief bio
  - **Services**: What you offer, pricing ranges
  - **Case Studies**: Your 3 case studies
- [ ] Add contact form or [Calendly](https://calendly.com) booking link
- [ ] Connect custom domain (optional): [Namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com) ($10-15/year)

**Tools**:
- [Carrd](https://carrd.co)
- [Calendly](https://calendly.com) (free tier)
- [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) for free images

**Success Criteria**: Simple website live with case studies

---

### Day 7: Set Up Business Infrastructure
**Time**: 2-3 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Create business email: `yourname@yourdomain.com` or use [Google Workspace](https://workspace.google.com) ($6/month)
- [ ] Set up invoicing: [Wave](https://www.waveapps.com) (free) or [FreshBooks](https://www.freshbooks.com) ($15/month)
- [ ] Create contract template: [Bonsai](https://www.hellobonsai.com) ($24/month) or [free template](https://www.docracy.com/doc/showalluserdocs?sortBy=4&page=1&userId=7435)
- [ ] Set up [Calendly](https://calendly.com) for discovery calls
- [ ] Create simple proposal template in Google Docs

**Tools**:
- [Wave](https://www.waveapps.com) - Free invoicing
- [Calendly](https://calendly.com) - Free scheduling
- [Bonsai](https://www.hellobonsai.com) - Contracts & proposals
- [DocuSign](https://www.docusign.com) - E-signatures (free tier)

**Success Criteria**: Business systems ready to onboard clients

---

## Week 2: Platform Setup & Outreach (Days 8-14)

### Day 8: Create Upwork Profile
**Time**: 2-3 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Sign up at [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com)
- [ ] Complete profile 100%:
  - Professional photo
  - Compelling title: "Senior DevOps Engineer | Kubernetes & Terraform Expert"
  - Overview: Focus on client outcomes, not just skills
  - Portfolio: Link to case studies and GitHub
  - Skills: Add 10-15 relevant skills
- [ ] Take Upwork skill tests (optional): Kubernetes, AWS, Linux
- [ ] Set hourly rate: Start at $75-100/hr to build reviews

**Tools**:
- [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com)
- [Upwork Profile Tips](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211068358-Create-a-Profile)

**Success Criteria**: Complete Upwork profile live

---

### Day 9: Apply to Toptal (Optional, Long-term)
**Time**: 1-2 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Apply at [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/developers)
- [ ] Complete initial application
- [ ] Note: Vetting process takes 2-4 weeks, continue with other platforms

**Tools**:
- [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/developers)

**Success Criteria**: Application submitted

---

### Day 10: Submit First 5 Upwork Proposals
**Time**: 3-4 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Search for projects matching your niche
- [ ] Filter: Entry level, $500-2,000 budget (build reviews first)
- [ ] Write custom proposals (no templates!) using this structure:

```markdown
Hi [Client Name],

I noticed you're looking to [specific problem from their post]. I've solved this exact issue for [similar client].

Here's how I'd approach it:
1. [Step 1 - be specific]
2. [Step 2]
3. [Step 3]

Timeline: [X days]
Rate: $[Y]/hr (or fixed price)

I've attached a [sample architecture diagram/relevant case study] showing a similar solution.

Looking forward to discussing this further.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

- [ ] Submit 5 proposals
- [ ] Track in spreadsheet: Project, Date, Response

**Tools**:
- [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com)
- Google Sheets for tracking

**Success Criteria**: 5 personalized proposals submitted

---

### Day 11: Submit 5 More Upwork Proposals
**Time**: 2-3 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Submit 5 more proposals (total: 10)
- [ ] Vary your approach based on project type
- [ ] Include portfolio links in every proposal

**Success Criteria**: 10 total proposals submitted

---

### Day 12: Direct Outreach - Build Target List
**Time**: 2-3 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Use [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com) to find recently funded startups
- [ ] Filter: Series A-B, 20-100 employees, your target industry
- [ ] Create list of 20 companies
- [ ] Find CTOs/VPs of Engineering on LinkedIn
- [ ] Note: Connection requests, not cold messages yet

**Tools**:
- [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com)
- [LinkedIn Sales Navigator](https://business.linkedin.com/sales-solutions/sales-navigator) (free trial)
- Google Sheets for tracking

**Success Criteria**: List of 20 target companies with decision-makers

---

### Day 13: LinkedIn Outreach - Connect with 10 Prospects
**Time**: 2 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Send personalized connection requests to 10 CTOs/VPs
- [ ] Use this template:

```
Hi [Name],

I noticed [Company] recently [raised funding/launched product]. Congrats!

I specialize in helping companies at your stage [specific outcome - e.g., "optimize Kubernetes costs"]. Would love to connect and share insights from working with [similar companies].

Best,
[Your Name]
```

- [ ] Don't pitch yet - just connect

**Success Criteria**: 10 connection requests sent

---

### Day 14: Content Marketing - First LinkedIn Post
**Time**: 1-2 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Write and publish your first LinkedIn post
- [ ] Topic ideas:
  - "5 Kubernetes Mistakes I See Startups Make"
  - "How We Reduced AWS Costs by 60% [Case Study]"
  - "The Hidden Costs of DIY Infrastructure"
- [ ] Include a call-to-action: "Facing similar challenges? Let's chat."
- [ ] Engage with comments for 1 hour after posting

**Success Criteria**: First LinkedIn post published, 10+ reactions

---

## Week 3: Proposals & Discovery Calls (Days 15-21)

### Day 15: Submit 5 More Upwork Proposals
**Time**: 2-3 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Submit 5 more proposals (total: 15)
- [ ] Target slightly larger projects: $2k-5k
- [ ] Reference your growing portfolio and any responses

**Success Criteria**: 15 total proposals submitted

---

### Day 16: Follow Up on Upwork Proposals
**Time**: 1-2 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Review proposals from Days 10-11
- [ ] Send polite follow-ups if no response:

```
Hi [Client],

Just following up on my proposal. I've helped [similar company] with [similar problem] and would love to discuss how I can help with [their project].

Happy to jump on a quick call if that's easier.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**Success Criteria**: Follow-ups sent for 5-10 proposals

---

### Day 17: Prepare for Discovery Calls
**Time**: 2 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Create discovery call script:
  - Introduction (2 min)
  - Understand their problem (10 min)
  - Share relevant experience (5 min)
  - Discuss approach (8 min)
  - Next steps (5 min)
- [ ] Prepare questions:
  - "What's your biggest infrastructure challenge right now?"
  - "What happens if this isn't solved?"
  - "What's your timeline and budget?"
- [ ] Set up Zoom or Google Meet

**Tools**:
- [Zoom](https://zoom.us) (free tier)
- [Google Meet](https://meet.google.com)

**Success Criteria**: Discovery call script ready

---

### Day 18-20: Conduct Discovery Calls
**Time**: 1-2 hours per day

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Conduct 3-5 discovery calls (from Upwork responses or LinkedIn)
- [ ] Take detailed notes during calls
- [ ] Send follow-up email within 24 hours:

```
Hi [Name],

Great chatting today! Based on our conversation, here's what I heard:

**Challenge**: [Their problem]
**Goal**: [Desired outcome]
**Timeline**: [When they need it]

I'll send over a detailed proposal by [date] outlining:
- Approach and timeline
- Deliverables
- Pricing

Looking forward to working together!

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**Success Criteria**: 3-5 discovery calls completed, follow-ups sent

---

### Day 21: Create 2-3 Detailed Proposals
**Time**: 3-4 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Write detailed proposals for your best discovery calls
- [ ] Use this structure:

```markdown
# Proposal: [Project Name]

## Executive Summary
[One paragraph: problem, solution, outcome]

## Scope of Work
**Phase 1**: [Deliverable 1]
**Phase 2**: [Deliverable 2]
**Phase 3**: [Deliverable 3]

## Timeline
- Week 1-2: [Phase 1]
- Week 3-4: [Phase 2]
- Week 5-6: [Phase 3]

## Pricing
**Option 1**: Fixed Price - $[X]
**Option 2**: Hourly - $[Y]/hr (estimated [Z] hours)

## Next Steps
1. Review and approve proposal
2. Sign contract
3. Kickoff call on [date]

Looking forward to working together!

[Your Name]
[Contact Info]
```

**Tools**:
- Google Docs or [Bonsai](https://www.hellobonsai.com)

**Success Criteria**: 2-3 detailed proposals sent

## Week 4: Close Deals & Deliver (Days 22-30)

### Day 22-24: Follow Up on Proposals
**Time**: 1-2 hours per day

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Follow up on proposals after 2-3 days
- [ ] Offer to answer questions or adjust scope
- [ ] Be prepared to negotiate (10-20% discount for first clients is okay)

**Success Criteria**: Active conversations with 2-3 prospects

---

### Day 25: Close First Client
**Time**: 2-3 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Finalize contract with first client
- [ ] Send contract via [DocuSign](https://www.docusign.com) or [HelloSign](https://www.hellosign.com)
- [ ] Set up payment:
  - 50% upfront, 50% on completion (for small projects)
  - Weekly/bi-weekly invoices (for hourly)
- [ ] Send invoice for deposit
- [ ] Schedule kickoff call

**Success Criteria**: First contract signed, deposit received

---

### Day 26-28: Start First Project
**Time**: Variable (depends on project)

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Conduct kickoff call
- [ ] Set up communication channels (Slack, email)
- [ ] Begin work on first deliverable
- [ ] Send daily or every-other-day updates
- [ ] Over-communicate progress

**Success Criteria**: Project started, client happy with communication

---

### Day 29: Continue Outreach (Don't Stop!)
**Time**: 2-3 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Submit 5 more Upwork proposals
- [ ] Post another LinkedIn update about your work
- [ ] Connect with 10 more prospects
- [ ] Goal: Build pipeline while delivering

**Success Criteria**: Pipeline stays full

---

### Day 30: Reflect & Plan Next 30 Days
**Time**: 1-2 hours

**Tasks**:
- [ ] Review your progress:
  - How many proposals sent?
  - How many discovery calls?
  - How many clients signed?
  - Revenue generated?
- [ ] Identify what worked and what didn't
- [ ] Plan next 30 days:
  - Increase rates by 10-20%
  - Target larger projects
  - Ask for referrals from current clients
- [ ] Celebrate your wins! 🎉

**Success Criteria**: 2-3 clients, $2k-5k revenue, clear plan for next month

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Start with smaller projects** ($500-2k) to build 5-star reviews, then increase rates
2. **Respond to Upwork jobs within 1 hour** of posting for better visibility
3. **Ask for testimonials** after every successful project
4. **Raise your rates every 3 months** as you gain experience and reviews
5. **Don't compete on price** - compete on expertise and outcomes

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The #1 Mistake**: Waiting to be "ready." Start submitting proposals on Day 10 even if your profile isn't perfect. You'll learn more from real conversations than from preparation.

> [!TIP]
> **Quick Win**: On Day 1, reach out to a former colleague or manager and ask if they know anyone who needs DevOps help. Referrals convert 10x better than cold outreach.
