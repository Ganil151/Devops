# 30-Day Action Plan: Launch Your Content Creation Business

Generate $1,200-3,200 from paid writing and build an audience of 100+ subscribers in 30 days.

---

## 📊 30-Day Overview

| Week | Focus | Goal |
|------|-------|------|
| **Week 1** | Niche & Platform Research | Niche defined, 3 platforms identified |
| **Week 2** | Applications & First Articles | 2 platforms accepted, 2 articles written |
| **Week 3** | Blog Launch & Audience Building | Blog live, 4 posts published, 50 subscribers |
| **Week 4** | Scale Content & Revenue | 4 paid articles published, 100+ subscribers |

---

## Week 1: Foundation (Days 1-7)

### Day 1: Choose Your Content Niche
- [ ] Review high-demand niches: Platform Engineering, FinOps, GitOps, Observability
- [ ] Choose ONE specific niche
- [ ] List 10 article ideas in your niche
- [ ] Research competition on [Dev.to](https://dev.to) and [Medium](https://medium.com)

**Tools**: Notion, Google Docs

---

### Day 2-3: Research Paid Writing Platforms
- [ ] Read 10 articles on each platform:
  - [DigitalOcean Community](https://www.digitalocean.com/community/pages/write-for-digitalocean)
  - [LogRocket Blog](https://blog.logrocket.com/become-a-logrocket-guest-author/)
  - [Honeybadger](https://www.honeybadger.io/blog/write-for-us/)
- [ ] Note: Tone, structure, code example format
- [ ] Identify 3 content gaps you can fill

**Tools**: Browser bookmarks, Notion for notes

---

### Day 4-5: Craft 3 Article Pitches
- [ ] Write detailed pitches for 3 different platforms
- [ ] Use this template:

```markdown
Subject: Article Pitch: [Specific Topic]

Hi [Editor Name],

I'd like to propose an article for [Publication]:

**Title**: "[How to X with Y: A Production Guide]"
**Target Audience**: [Specific audience]
**Outline**:
1. [Section 1]
2. [Section 2]
3. [Section 3]
4. [Section 4]
5. [Section 5]

**Why I'm Qualified**: [Your experience]
**Estimated Length**: [Word count]
**Code Examples**: [What you'll include]

Available to start immediately.

Best,
[Your Name]
[Portfolio/GitHub Link]
```

---

### Day 6: Submit Pitches
- [ ] Submit pitches to 3 platforms
- [ ] Track in spreadsheet: Platform, Date, Topic, Response

**Tools**: Gmail, Google Sheets

---

### Day 7: Set Up Blog
- [ ] Choose platform: [Hashnode](https://hashnode.com) (recommended), [Ghost](https://ghost.org), or [Dev.to](https://dev.to)
- [ ] Set up custom domain (optional): [Namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com)
- [ ] Configure SEO settings
- [ ] Add About page and social links

**Tools**: [Hashnode](https://hashnode.com) (free), [Namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com) ($10/year)

---

## Week 2: Write & Publish (Days 8-14)

### Day 8-10: Write First Paid Article
- [ ] Choose accepted pitch or write spec article
- [ ] Structure: Hook → Context → Tutorial → Troubleshooting → Conclusion
- [ ] Include 3-5 code examples (tested!)
- [ ] Add diagrams using [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com)
- [ ] Proofread with [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com)

**Tools**: [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com), [Hemingway Editor](http://hemingwayapp.com/), [Carbon](https://carbon.now.sh) for code screenshots

---

### Day 11: Submit First Article
- [ ] Final review and submit
- [ ] Follow submission guidelines exactly
- [ ] Include author bio and headshot

---

### Day 12-14: Write First Blog Post
- [ ] Choose SEO-friendly topic using [AnswerThePublic](https://answerthepublic.com)
- [ ] Write 1,500-2,500 words
- [ ] Optimize for SEO:
  - Title with keyword
  - Meta description
  - Headers (H2, H3)
  - Internal/external links
- [ ] Publish on your blog

**Tools**: [AnswerThePublic](https://answerthepublic.com), [Ubersuggest](https://neilpatel.com/ubersuggest/)

---

## Week 3: Build Audience (Days 15-21)

### Day 15-17: Write 3 More Blog Posts
- [ ] Post 1: Tutorial (how-to guide)
- [ ] Post 2: Opinion piece (hot take on DevOps trend)
- [ ] Post 3: Case study (real-world problem you solved)
- [ ] Publish one every other day

---

### Day 18: Set Up Email List
- [ ] Choose platform: [ConvertKit](https://convertkit.com) (free up to 1k subscribers) or [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com)
- [ ] Create lead magnet: "DevOps Cheat Sheet" or "Kubernetes Quick Reference"
- [ ] Add signup form to blog
- [ ] Create welcome email sequence

**Tools**: [ConvertKit](https://convertkit.com), [Canva](https://www.canva.com) for lead magnet design

---

### Day 19-20: Promote Your Content
- [ ] Share each blog post on:
  - LinkedIn (with commentary)
  - Twitter (thread format)
  - [Dev.to](https://dev.to) (cross-post)
  - Reddit: r/devops, r/kubernetes (follow rules!)
- [ ] Engage with comments for 1 hour after posting

**Tools**: [Buffer](https://buffer.com) or [Hootsuite](https://hootsuite.com) for scheduling

---

### Day 21: Write Second Paid Article
- [ ] Write and submit second paid article
- [ ] Target different platform than first

**Goal**: 50+ email subscribers by end of week

---

## Week 4: Scale Revenue (Days 22-30)

### Day 22-24: Write Third & Fourth Paid Articles
- [ ] Write two more paid articles
- [ ] Submit to platforms that accepted your pitches
- [ ] Follow up on pending pitches

---

### Day 25-26: Launch Newsletter
- [ ] Write first newsletter issue
- [ ] Format: Weekly tips, tutorial, tool recommendation
- [ ] Send to your list
- [ ] Promote on social media

---

### Day 27: Apply to More Platforms
- [ ] Apply to 3 additional paid writing platforms:
  - [Twilio Blog](https://www.twilio.com/blog)
  - [CircleCI Blog](https://circleci.com/blog/)
  - [Auth0 Blog](https://auth0.com/blog/)

---

### Day 28-29: Content Repurposing
- [ ] Turn best blog post into:
  - LinkedIn carousel (10 slides)
  - Twitter thread
  - YouTube video script (for future)
- [ ] Publish repurposed content

**Tools**: [Canva](https://www.canva.com) for carousel design

---

### Day 30: Reflect & Plan
- [ ] Review metrics:
  - Articles published: Target 4
  - Revenue: $1,200-3,200
  - Email subscribers: 100+
  - Blog traffic: 500+ visitors
- [ ] Plan next 30 days:
  - Increase article rate (6-8/month)
  - Grow list to 500 subscribers
  - Start YouTube channel or podcast

---

## 📊 Success Metrics

By Day 30:
- ✅ 4 paid articles published ($1,200-3,200)
- ✅ Blog with 5+ posts
- ✅ 100+ email subscribers
- ✅ Active on LinkedIn and Twitter
- ✅ 2-3 platforms accepting your work
