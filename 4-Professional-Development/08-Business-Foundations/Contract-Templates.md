## <a name="msa"></a>📄 Master Services Agreement (MSA)

```markdown
# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**This Master Services Agreement** ("Agreement") is entered into as of [DATE] ("Effective Date")

**BETWEEN:**
[YOUR COMPANY NAME] LLC
[YOUR ADDRESS]
("Consultant")

**AND:**
[CLIENT COMPANY NAME]
[CLIENT ADDRESS]
("Client")

## 1. SERVICES

Consultant agrees to provide DevOps consulting services as described in individual Statements of Work ("SOW") executed under this Agreement.

## 2. TERM AND TERMINATION

2.1 **Term**: This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated by either party.

2.2 **Termination for Convenience**: Either party may terminate this Agreement with thirty (30) days written notice.

2.3 **Termination for Cause**: Either party may terminate immediately if the other party:
   - Breaches any material term and fails to cure within fifteen (15) days
   - Becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy
   - Engages in illegal or unethical conduct

2.4 **Effect of Termination**: Client shall pay for all services performed up to termination date.

## 3. COMPENSATION AND PAYMENT

3.1 **Rates**: Consultant's rates are specified in each SOW.

3.2 **Invoicing**: Consultant will invoice Client [weekly/bi-weekly/monthly] for services performed.

3.3 **Payment Terms**: Payment is due within thirty (30) days of invoice date ("Net 30").

3.4 **Late Payments**: Late payments will incur interest at 1.5% per month (18% annually) or the maximum allowed by law.

3.5 **Expenses**: Client will reimburse pre-approved expenses with receipts.

## 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

4.1 **Client Materials**: All materials provided by Client remain Client's property.

4.2 **Deliverables**: Upon full payment, Client owns all custom deliverables created specifically for Client under an SOW.

4.3 **Pre-Existing Materials**: Consultant retains ownership of:
   - Pre-existing tools, scripts, and methodologies
   - General knowledge and experience
   - Reusable components not specific to Client

4.4 **License**: Consultant grants Client a non-exclusive, perpetual license to use any pre-existing materials incorporated into deliverables.

## 5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 **Definition**: "Confidential Information" includes business plans, technical data, customer lists, source code, and any information marked confidential.

5.2 **Obligations**: Both parties agree to:
   - Keep Confidential Information strictly confidential
   - Use it only for purposes of this Agreement
   - Not disclose to third parties without written consent

5.3 **Exceptions**: Confidential Information does not include information that:
   - Is publicly available through no breach of this Agreement
   - Was known prior to disclosure
   - Is independently developed
   - Is required to be disclosed by law

5.4 **Duration**: Obligations continue for three (3) years after termination.

## 6. WARRANTIES AND DISCLAIMERS

6.1 **Consultant Warranties**: Consultant warrants that:
   - Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner
   - Deliverables will conform to specifications in the SOW
   - Has the right to enter into this Agreement

6.2 **DISCLAIMER**: EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED, CONSULTANT PROVIDES SERVICES "AS IS" WITHOUT WARRANTIES OF ANY KIND, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

7.1 **Cap on Liability**: CONSULTANT'S TOTAL LIABILITY SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID BY CLIENT IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

7.2 **Excluded Damages**: IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS.

7.3 **Exceptions**: Limitations do not apply to:
   - Gross negligence or willful misconduct
   - Breach of confidentiality
   - Indemnification obligations

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 **By Consultant**: Consultant will indemnify Client against claims that deliverables infringe third-party intellectual property rights.

8.2 **By Client**: Client will indemnify Consultant against claims arising from:
   - Client's use of deliverables beyond scope of license
   - Client's breach of this Agreement
   - Client-provided materials or instructions

## 9. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR

9.1 **Relationship**: Consultant is an independent contractor, not an employee.

9.2 **No Benefits**: Consultant is not entitled to employee benefits.

9.3 **Taxes**: Consultant is responsible for all taxes.

9.4 **Control**: Consultant controls how services are performed.

## 10. GENERAL PROVISIONS

10.1 **Entire Agreement**: This Agreement and all SOWs constitute the entire agreement.

10.2 **Amendments**: Must be in writing and signed by both parties.

10.3 **Governing Law**: [YOUR STATE] law governs this Agreement.

10.4 **Dispute Resolution**: Disputes will be resolved through binding arbitration in [YOUR CITY, STATE] under AAA Commercial Arbitration Rules.

10.5 **Severability**: If any provision is invalid, the remainder continues in effect.

10.6 **Assignment**: Neither party may assign without written consent.

10.7 **Notices**: Must be in writing to addresses above.

**AGREED AND ACCEPTED:**

**CONSULTANT:**
[YOUR COMPANY NAME] LLC

By: _________________________
Name: [YOUR NAME]
Title: [YOUR TITLE]
Date: _______________________

**CLIENT:**
[CLIENT COMPANY NAME]

By: _________________________
Name: _______________________
Title: _______________________
Date: _______________________
```

```markdown
[CLIENT COMPANY NAME] ("Client")

- Assess current infrastructure and architecture
- Document existing CI/CD pipelines
- Identify pain points and optimization opportunities
- Create migration/optimization roadmap

**Deliverables**:
- Current state assessment document
- Recommended architecture diagram
- Implementation roadmap with timeline

### Phase 2: Implementation (Weeks 3-6)
- [Specific task 1]
- [Specific task 2]
- [Specific task 3]

**Deliverables**:
- [Deliverable 1]
- [Deliverable 2]
- [Deliverable 3]

### Phase 3: Testing & Documentation (Weeks 7-8)
- End-to-end testing in staging environment
- Performance and load testing
- Create runbooks and documentation
- Knowledge transfer session with team

**Deliverables**:
- Test results and sign-off
- Complete documentation package
- Recorded training session

## 3. OUT OF SCOPE

The following are explicitly excluded from this SOW:
- [Item 1]
- [Item 2]
- [Item 3]

**Change Orders**: Any changes to scope require written change order and may affect timeline and budget.

## 4. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

Client agrees to provide:
- Access to necessary systems and environments
- Timely responses to questions (within 2 business days)
- Designated point of contact
- Required approvals within agreed timeframes

## 5. TIMELINE

| Milestone | Deliverable | Due Date |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Kickoff | Project plan | Week 1 |
| Phase 1 Complete | Assessment document | Week 2 |
| Phase 2 Complete | Implementation | Week 6 |

- 50% ($[AMOUNT]) upon signing this SOW
- 25% ($[AMOUNT]) upon Phase 2 completion
- 25% ($[AMOUNT]) upon final acceptance

## 8. EXPENSES

Pre-approved expenses will be reimbursed:
- Cloud infrastructure costs (AWS, GCP, Azure)
- Third-party tools or licenses required for project
- Travel expenses (if applicable)

## 9. ASSUMPTIONS

- [Assumption 1]
- [Assumption 2]
- [Assumption 3]

If assumptions prove incorrect, timeline and budget may be adjusted.

## 10. RISKS

Identified risks and mitigation strategies:
- **Risk**: [Description]
  **Mitigation**: [Strategy]

## 11. SUCCESS METRICS

Project success will be measured by:
- [Metric 1: e.g., "Deployment time reduced by 50%"]
- [Metric 2: e.g., "Zero production incidents during migration"]
- [Metric 3: e.g., "Team able to independently manage system"]

**CONSULTANT:**
[YOUR COMPANY NAME] LLC

By: _________________________

[CLIENT COMPANY NAME]
```

## <a name="nda"></a>🔒 Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

```markdown
# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

**This Agreement** is entered into as of [DATE]

**BETWEEN:**
[YOUR COMPANY NAME] LLC ("Disclosing Party" / "Receiving Party")
**AND:**
[CLIENT COMPANY NAME] ("Disclosing Party" / "Receiving Party")

(Each a "Party" and collectively the "Parties")

## 1. PURPOSE

The Parties wish to explore a business relationship regarding [BRIEF DESCRIPTION] (the "Purpose").

## 2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

2.1 **Definition**: "Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one Party to the other, whether orally, in writing, or electronically, including but not limited to:
   - Business plans and strategies
   - Technical data and specifications
   - Source code and algorithms
   - Customer and supplier lists
   - Financial information
   - Product roadmaps
   - Any information marked "Confidential"

2.2 **Exclusions**: Confidential Information does not include information that:
   - Is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement
   - Was known to Receiving Party prior to disclosure
   - Is independently developed by Receiving Party
   - Is rightfully received from a third party without confidentiality obligations
   - Must be disclosed by law (with prior notice to Disclosing Party)

## 3. OBLIGATIONS

3.1 **Non-Disclosure**: Receiving Party agrees to:
   - Keep Confidential Information strictly confidential
   - Not disclose to any third party without written consent
   - Use same degree of care as for own confidential information (minimum reasonable care)

3.2 **Use Limitation**: Use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose.

3.3 **Need-to-Know**: Disclose only to employees, contractors, and advisors who:
   - Have a legitimate need to know
   - Are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective

## 4. TERM

4.1 **Duration**: This Agreement is effective from the date above and continues for two (2) years.

4.2 **Survival**: Confidentiality obligations survive termination for three (3) years.

## 5. RETURN OF MATERIALS

Upon request or termination, Receiving Party will:
- Return or destroy all Confidential Information
- Certify destruction in writing

## 6. NO LICENSE

No license or rights are granted except as expressly stated. All intellectual property remains with Disclosing Party.

## 7. NO WARRANTY

Confidential Information is provided "AS IS" without warranty of any kind.

## 8. REMEDIES

8.1 **Irreparable Harm**: Breach may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages are inadequate.

8.2 **Injunctive Relief**: Disclosing Party may seek injunctive relief without posting bond.

## 9. GENERAL

9.1 **Governing Law**: [YOUR STATE] law governs this Agreement.

9.2 **Entire Agreement**: This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement regarding confidentiality.

9.3 **Amendments**: Must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

9.4 **Severability**: Invalid provisions do not affect the remainder.

**AGREED AND ACCEPTED:**

**PARTY 1:**
[YOUR COMPANY NAME] LLC

By: _________________________
Name: [YOUR NAME]
Date: _______________________

**PARTY 2:**
[CLIENT COMPANY NAME]

By: _________________________
Name: _______________________
Date: _______________________
```

## <a name="remote-work"></a>🌍 Remote Work Contract Addendum

Use this addendum with your MSA or Independent Contractor Agreement when working remotely.

```markdown
# REMOTE WORK ADDENDUM

**This Addendum** modifies the [Master Services Agreement / Independent Contractor Agreement] 
dated [DATE] between [YOUR COMPANY NAME] LLC ("Contractor") and [CLIENT COMPANY NAME] ("Client").

## 1. REMOTE WORK ARRANGEMENT

1.1 **Work Location**: Contractor shall perform services remotely from:
   - Primary Location: [CITY, STATE/COUNTRY]
   - Contractor may work from other locations with notice to Client

1.2 **No On-Site Requirement**: This engagement does not require physical presence 
at Client's facilities except as mutually agreed in writing.

1.3 **Travel**: If on-site presence is requested:
   - Minimum [X] days advance notice required
   - Client will reimburse all travel expenses (flights, lodging, meals)
   - Travel time is billable at [50% / 100%] of standard rate

## 2. WORKING HOURS AND AVAILABILITY

2.1 **Core Hours**: Contractor will be available during:
   - [TIME] to [TIME] [TIMEZONE] on business days
   - This represents [X] hours of guaranteed overlap

2.2 **Response Times**:
   - Slack/Email: Within [4] hours during core hours
   - Urgent matters: Within [1] hour during core hours
   - After hours: Next business day (unless emergency)

2.3 **Meetings**: Contractor will accommodate meetings within core hours.
   Meetings outside core hours require [24-48] hours advance notice.

2.4 **Timezone**: All deadlines and commitments reference [TIMEZONE] unless 
   otherwise specified.

## 3. EQUIPMENT AND WORKSPACE

3.1 **Equipment**: Contractor provides all equipment, including:
   - Computer hardware and peripherals
   - Software and development tools
   - Internet connectivity and backup

3.2 **Expenses**: The following are Contractor's responsibility:
   - Home office setup and utilities
   - Internet service
   - Equipment maintenance and upgrades

3.3 **Client-Provided**: Client may provide:
   - Access to Client's software and systems
   - Licenses for Client-specific tools
   - VPN and security credentials

## 4. COMMUNICATION

4.1 **Primary Channels**:
   - Synchronous: [Slack / Microsoft Teams / Zoom]
   - Asynchronous: [Email / Project Management Tool]
   - Video Calls: [Zoom / Google Meet / Teams]

4.2 **Status Updates**: Contractor will provide:
   - Daily async standups in [platform]
   - Weekly summary reports
   - Immediate notification of blockers

4.3 **Camera Policy**: Camera use during video calls is:
   - [ ] Required for all meetings
   - [X] Encouraged but not required
   - [ ] Optional

## 5. DATA SECURITY

5.1 **Security Requirements**: Contractor agrees to:
   - Use encrypted storage for all Client data
   - Maintain password-protected devices
   - Use VPN when accessing Client systems
   - Enable full-disk encryption
   - Use multi-factor authentication

5.2 **Physical Security**:
   - Work from secure, private locations
   - Prevent unauthorized viewing of screens
   - Lock devices when unattended
   - Secure disposal of printed materials

5.3 **Network Security**:
   - No use of public WiFi for Client work without VPN
   - Maintain updated antivirus/security software
   - Report any security incidents immediately

5.4 **Data Handling**:
   - No Client data on personal devices (if separate work device required)
   - All data deleted/returned upon project completion
   - No data stored in unapproved cloud services

## 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY (Remote Specifics)

6.1 **Work Product**: All work product created under this Agreement 
   is subject to IP terms in the underlying Agreement.

6.2 **Personal Projects**: Contractor may work on personal projects 
   outside of Client hours, provided:
   - No Client resources are used
   - No conflict with Client's business
   - No Client Confidential Information is incorporated

## 7. COMPLIANCE

7.1 **Tax Compliance**: Contractor remains solely responsible for:
   - All income taxes in Contractor's jurisdiction
   - Self-employment taxes
   - Any local business licenses or registrations

7.2 **Legal Compliance**: Contractor will comply with all applicable 
   laws in Contractor's jurisdiction.

7.3 **Export Controls**: Contractor will not export Client data or 
   technology in violation of US export control laws.

## 8. TERM

This Addendum is effective from [DATE] and continues for the duration 
of the underlying Agreement unless terminated earlier.
```

## 💡 Tips for Using These Templates

### Customization Checklist
- [ ] Replace all [BRACKETED] placeholders
- [ ] Adjust payment terms to your preference
- [ ] Add your state's specific legal requirements
- [ ] Have attorney review before first use
- [ ] Update annually for law changes

### Best Practices
1. **Always Use Written Contracts**: Never start work without signed agreement
2. **Get Signatures**: Use DocuSign, HelloSign, or Adobe Sign
3. **Keep Copies**: Maintain organized digital and physical copies
4. **Review Regularly**: Update templates annually
5. **Consult Attorney**: For high-value or complex projects

### Red Flags to Avoid
- ❌ Unlimited liability
- ❌ Work-for-hire without extra compensation
- ❌ Net 90+ payment terms
- ❌ No termination clause
- ❌ Vague scope of work
