# 🚀 DevOps Career Prompt Engineering Arsenal

## Overview

This directory contains battle-tested AI prompts designed to **accelerate your DevOps career**. Each prompt is engineered to extract maximum value from AI assistants when preparing for job applications, interviews, and resume optimization.

---

## 📁 Available Prompts

### 1. **High-Octane DevOps Recruiter** (`recruiter-prompt.md`)
**Purpose:** Transform your resume with recruiter-level insights  
**Use When:** You have a target job description and need to maximize your match score  
**Output:** Match score, keyword gap analysis, rewritten experience section

### 2. **ATS Stress Test** (`ats-stress-test.md`)
**Purpose:** Identify resume formatting vulnerabilities before ATS systems reject you  
**Use When:** You've updated your resume and need to ensure it's ATS-compliant  
**Output:** Risk report highlighting parsing blockers and structural issues

### 3. **Final Round Interview** (`final-round-interview.md`)
**Purpose:** Prepare for senior-level scenario-based technical interviews  
**Use When:** You've landed a final round interview with engineering leadership  
**Output:** Top 3 hardest questions + STAR-method answers + red flags to avoid

---

## 🎯 How to Use These Prompts

1. **Copy the entire prompt** from the specific `.md` file
2. **Fill in the placeholders** (e.g., `[PASTE JD HERE]`, `[PASTE RESUME HERE]`)
3. **Paste into your AI assistant** (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.)
4. **Iterate based on feedback** - these prompts are designed for conversation

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Chain the prompts:** Use the Recruiter → ATS → Interview sequence for comprehensive preparation
- **Save the outputs:** Store AI-generated insights in version control alongside your resume
- **Update regularly:** Re-run these prompts as you gain new experience or target different roles
- **Customize the role:** Adjust the "Act as..." persona for different industries (FinTech, Healthcare, etc.)
