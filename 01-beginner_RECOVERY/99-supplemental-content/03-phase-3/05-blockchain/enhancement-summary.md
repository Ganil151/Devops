## 🏗️ Technical Standards (The "Web3 Ops Way")

Every module in this phase must follow these standards:

1.  **Junior vs. Engineer Comparison**: Contrast "Wallet users" with "Node operators."
2.  **Infrastructure Analogies**: Use models like "The Digital Notary," "The Distributed Truth," and "The Immutable History."
3.  **Visual Architecture**: Use Mermaid diagrams for block propagation and consensus mechanisms.
4.  **Security-First Coding**: Emphasize private key management, slashing protection, and DDoS mitigation.
5.  **Observability Patterns**: Focus on block height, peer counts, and JSON-RPC health.
