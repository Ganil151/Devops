2.  **Infrastructure Analogies**: Use models like "The Utility Bill," "The Hotel Standby," and "The Metered Taxi."
3.  **Visual Architecture**: Use Mermaid diagrams for the Inform-Optimize-Operate cycle and Tagging hierarchies.
4.  **Unit Economics Focus**: Move beyond "Total Bill" to "Cost per Business Outcome."
5.  **Fail-Fast/Alert-Early**: Emphasize proactive alerting over monthly bill shocks.
